# Walk-through video — recording script

A screen recording of the app on a phone, roughly **2½ minutes**. Everything
below is already in the app, so nothing needs setting up first.

**Before you record**

- Turn on Do Not Disturb so notifications stay out of the frame.
- Start from the browser, not the installed app — the first scene is installing it.
- iPhone: Settings → Control Centre → add Screen Recording. Android: pull down
  the shade → Screen record.
- Record in portrait. Speak over it afterwards if you prefer, rather than while
  recording, so the room stays quiet.

---

## 1 · Installing it — 25s

> "You don't get this from the App Store. You open a link, and save it to your
> phone."

Open the link in Safari (or Chrome). Let the splash play — the book draws
itself, then the contents appear.

- **iPhone:** tap Share → scroll → **Add to Home Screen** → Add.
- **Android:** tap ⋮ → **Install app**.

Close the browser. Tap the new icon on the home screen.

> "Now it opens like any other app, and it works with no signal."

## 2 · Finding a hymn — 30s

> "Three ways to find a hymn."

- Type `amazing` — the title appears.
- Clear it, type `streams of mercy` — 334 appears with the matching line
  underneath in italics.

> "You don't need the title. A line you half remember is enough."

- Tap **No.**, key in `300`, press Open.

> "Or the number, if you already know it."

## 3 · Reading and languages — 20s

Scroll through Rock of Ages. Point out the verse labels.

Tap **Yorùbá** under the title.

> "The same hymn in Yorùbá. It finds the right number on its own — the
> Yorùbá hymnal numbers them differently."

Tap the heart.

> "That saves it to Favourites."

## 4 · The music — 25s

Press play.

> "That's the whole hymn, every verse. The first time you play it, it
> downloads — after that it works with no signal at all. The ring shows how
> far through it is."

Let it play a few seconds, then tap back.

> "Leaving the hymn stops the music."

## 5 · Putting it on a screen — 30s

Open a hymn and tap the **screen icon**.

> "This is for church. One verse at a time, big enough to read from the back.
> Tap or use the arrows to move through it."

- **Android:** tap **Cast**, pick the TV, show the words appearing on it.
  > "The TV shows the words. The phone stays the remote."
- **iPhone:** open Control Centre → Screen Mirroring → pick the TV.
  > "On iPhone you mirror the screen instead — Apple doesn't let a web app
  > cast on its own. AirPlay carries the music across too."

## 6 · Sharing — 20s

Tap the **share icon**. Pick a verse, then a colour. Tap **Share image**.

> "Pick the verse, pick a colour, send it to anyone."

## 7 · Making it yours — 20s

Go to **Settings**. Change the theme colour. Drag the lyrics size slider.

> "Four colours, light or dark, and you can make the words as large as you
> need. There's also a How to use it section in here if you forget any of
> this."

## 8 · Close — 10s

> "All 920 — every hymn, and the calls to worship, invocations and
> benedictions that most apps leave out. It's free, and it works offline.
> Share the link with anyone."

Send it alongside the link. WhatsApp compresses video hard, so if it looks
soft, upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** and share that link instead — unlisted
